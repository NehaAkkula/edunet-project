# HomeFood Connect - Product Walkthrough

HomeFood Connect is a premium, production-ready platform connecting home chefs with customers seeking authentic homemade food. The application features a stunning Indian-themed UI and a robust full-stack Django backend.

## 🎨 Premium UI/UX Design

The platform uses a sophisticated **Maroon (#800000)** and **Cream (#FFF5E1)** palette with **Gold** accents, creating a warm and authentic Indian culinary feel.

## 🍛 Dynamic Menu System

The menu features category-based filtering (All, Indian, Breakfast, Meals, etc.) with high-quality realistic food photography and real-time rating displays.

Customers can track their orders through a multi-step status flow (Ordered → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered) and provide valuable feedback through the rating system.

---

### Key Features Implemented:
- **Role-Based Authentication**: Separate signup/login flows for Customers and Homemakers.
- **Dynamic Filtering**: Pill-style category filters with smooth transitions.
- **Real-Time Ratings**: Automatic average rating calculation from customer reviews.
- **Robust Order Flow**: Full lifecycle management of food orders.
- **Premium Aesthetics**: Responsive design with hover effects, shadows, and elegant typography.
